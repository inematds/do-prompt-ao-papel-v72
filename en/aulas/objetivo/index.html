Lesson 5 · 15 min

A goal you can measure: the M in SMART

Managers have been delegating with SMART for half a century: specific, measurable, achievable, relevant and time-bound. For an agent, the letter that matters most is the M. Without a measure, it can't check its own work and doesn't know when it's finished. "Improve my website" is not a goal. "The home page opens in under two seconds on a phone" is.

Goal: turn a vague request into a goal with a measure and a deadline that the agent can check on its own.

01 · SMART for agents

Tap or click each letter. The middle one is the one that decides.

Quick test: if you can't say how you'll check it, the agent can't either.

The same method managers use with people. With an agent, the M stops being a detail and becomes the stopping criterion.

02 · From vague to measurable

Three requests rewritten. Tap or click each one.

1. Website

Vague: "Improve my website."

Measurable: the home page opens in under 2 seconds on a phone, measured three times in a row, by Friday.

How to checkPageSpeed Insights (pagespeed.web.dev, free), phone profile: three measurements under 2 s.

2. New law

Vague: "Summarize this new law for the clients."

Measurable: up to 10 lines, cites the law number and the date it takes effect, and says who is affected, today by 6 p.m.

How to checkCount the lines and find in the text the law number, the date and who is affected.

3. Announcement

Vague: "Improve the announcement to the parents."

Measurable: fits on one phone screen, gives the start date in the first sentence, and is approved with no request for corrections, by Friday at 4 p.m.

How to checkOpen it on a phone, find the date in the first sentence, and approve it or not.

Simulation: illustrative example
  1. The Measure has a number or a test, not an adjective.
  2. The Deadline says by when and how the result gets to you.
  3. You can tell, just by looking at what was delivered, whether it passed or not.
  • An adjective instead of a number ("fast", "pretty"): the agent decides on its own what that means.
  • A measure only you can run: prefer a test the agent runs itself.

    05 · Check your goal

    Paste the vague request and the rewritten version. The page checks the fields, rejects empty ones and loose adjectives, and asks for a Measure with a number or a test and a Deadline with a day, date or time. The OK only checks the format; it doesn't guarantee the measure is a good one: you decide.

    06 · Practice: your sheet's goal

    Go back to the sheet from lesson 4 and strengthen the Measure field.

    E1 · Three requests rewritten

    15 min

    Take three vague requests from your diagnosis (lesson 1) and rewrite each one using SMART.

    Done when: all three have a number or a test where an adjective used to be.

    E2 · The goal, checked

    10 min

    Write your agent's goal.txt and paste it into the checker above.

    Done when: the checker answers "OK: goal with all 4 fields filled in" and you wrote, in one sentence, how you check the Measure by looking at a delivery.

    E3 · The agent checks itself

    15 min

    Hand over the goal and ask it to tell you, when it finishes, how it checked each item of the Measure. If the measure calls for a tool it can't access (in a chat, PageSpeed, for example), you're the one who measures, using the same test.

    Done when: the delivery came with a check of each item, and you redid one of the tests and got the same result.

Lesson 8 · 14 min

The same task in the three eras, and your plan

The best proof that the way of working has changed is to do the same task three times: with just the prompt, with prompt and context, and with the full role. You see with your own eyes what each era delivers. Then you build the plan with dates to put your first agent to work, one step at a time.

    06 · The course in three sentences
    Stop hunting for the perfect promptDefine the role: a goal you can measure, context, and an example of your own.
    Train in cyclesSeveral rounds, a list of corrections and a written procedure. The same mistake doesn't come back.
    Watch the costThe right model, cache, batching and a bill you keep an eye on. There are no miracles: you can only relax once the system runs right.
